Hi! My name is Ally Venezia and I am a junior at Claremont McKenna College, where I am studying Philosophy and Public Affairs with a Sequence in Legal Studies. My coursework combines the disciplines of Philosophy with Government and Economics. I am interested in pursuing a career in law or public policy.

At CMC, I have had the opportunity to engage in a variety of academic and extracurricular activities that have shaped my interests and goals. For the past three years, I have been a member of the CMS Track and Field team, competing as a sprinter. Outside of athletics, I serve as a Student Manager for the Gould Center for Humanistic Studies and as Vice President of Path Consulting, experiences that have allowed me to further develop my leadership, research, and collaboration skills.
